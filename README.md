### [Metric dimension of random geometric graphs](https://github.com/rctillquist20/random-geometric-graphs/tree/main/local%20RGG)

My graduate research at Chico State focuses on finding small sets of landmarks whose graph distances uniquely identify every vertex. I implemented collision-based landmark selection, whole-graph repair, and reverse elimination. I built paired benchmarks to compare scoring and pruning methods across graph densities and clustered point clouds.

In the fine-radius sweep, all **325 resolving sets** passed exact validation across **65 graph instances** with 500 vertices each. [Benchmark outputs](https://github.com/rctillquist20/random-geometric-graphs/tree/main/local%20RGG/outputs) · [Shared-pruning benchmark](https://github.com/rctillquist20/random-geometric-graphs/blob/main/local%20RGG/outputs/shared_pruning_fine_radius_035_065.xlsx)
