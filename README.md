## Featured work

### [EvalGuard](https://github.com/adi0ri/EvalGuard)

I built a harness that tests whether tool-using AI agents follow policy and make the correct database changes. The credential-free demo runs 25 support cases across two sets of three trials and links each result to the underlying tool and grader evidence. [Demo and architecture](https://github.com/adi0ri/EvalGuard#run-the-demo)

### [Metric dimension of random geometric graphs](https://adi0ri.github.io/metric-dimension/)

Graduate research at Chico State. I implemented a whole-graph repair for landmark sets that leave pairs of vertices indistinguishable. In our paired benchmark, the Grid method produced valid resolving sets on **48 of 48 graphs**, up from **36 of 48**. [Merged contribution and results](https://github.com/rctillquist20/random-geometric-graphs/pull/1)

### [Seerlink](https://seerlink.seeryn.com/)

I co-built Seerlink, the internal app we use at Seeryn to find local businesses, audit their websites, and prioritize outreach. The hosted app requires sign-in.

### [Adaptive Skip Lists](https://github.com/adi0ri/adaptive-skip-lists)

My Master's research prototype uses a small neural network to adjust node promotion in response to access patterns. The notebook includes a synthetic hot-key workload and its saved trace.
